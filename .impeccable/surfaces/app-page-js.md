Mode: Operate. Scope: single guided-flow surface: city, stops, times, reels.
Audience and job: leisure traveler building a followable day plan; demoed on phone and desktop.
Content: three scored cities, ordered stops with per-leg times, live map, IG reels top five by views.

Direction: Poster Days, a WPA poster program rendered as guided plates. Memorable moment: the route band threading the stop list.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A trip day reads as a city poster program, not a card stack: each guided step is a plate in its city's flat ink, density carried by timetable strips and live headers. Refuses the rounded-widget travel template.

OWN-WORLD: Gallery-white ground, near-black ink, one saturated ink per city (red, blue, gold). Condensed grotesk display, tabular mono for times and counts, hairline rules, band dividers. Route as bold city-ink band; selected stop expands inline.

STORY: Traveler picks a city plate, builds stops with live times, watches reels, and leaves believing a day here plans itself.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead bar with wordmark and city switch; step rail 1 City, 2 Stops, 3 Times, 4 Reels with live counts; city plates full-bleed in city inks; selecting a city threads the route band into the stops plate below.
